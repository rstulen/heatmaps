How to use this map
Click a bus icon or circle for the stop's quality, ridership, shade, temperature and a Google Maps link.
Use the checkboxes to show or hide layers, and the sliders to fade the heat map, stops and circles.
Marker sizes follow the zoom level. Switch to Satellite (bottom left) to see what surrounds a stop.
Time of Day tab: load the LAVTA hourly CSV to chart boardings, alightings and bus loads by hour for any stop.
Bus capacity passengers (optional, to see how full)
